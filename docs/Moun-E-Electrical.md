## Moun E (Eco) electrical

Commodity lighting integration for Eco: low-risk, kit-first electrical scope.

### Scope

- Two commodity 5V USB adhesive LED strips kitted in-box.

- No custom PCBs, firmware, or CM electrical engineering in base scope.

- Pack-out verification and basic power-on audit only when required.

Running start impact

- NRE driver:  electrical NRE stays low because this scope avoids custom board and harness engineering.

- Landed driver:  per-unit electrical cost is mostly commodity strip COGS + kitting labor.

- Deferrable to scale:  stricter burn-in depth and advanced QC instrumentation can wait until 100/1000-unit runs.
