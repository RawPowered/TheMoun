## Modular Fixed-Geometry Digitization Workstation

Captures variant-ready modular architecture while preserving fixed-geometry behavior across Moun E and Moun E Cobra.

Claim emphasis

- Common geometry primitives across multiple chassis and accessory configurations.

- Tool-less module interchange without breaking repeatable optical alignment behavior.

- Family-level workstation framework to prevent near-copy variant cloning.
