## Fixed-Geometry Digitization Workstation

Covers workstation-level capture repeatability and deterministic geometry for high-consistency digitization output.

Claim emphasis

- Indexable Z-axis displacement for reproducible optical height and framing.

- Fixed capture posture for stable digitization outcomes across sessions and operators.

- Controlled illumination behavior coupled to geometry rather than ad-hoc external rigs.
