## Moun E (Eco) manufacturing

CM-first production scope for Eco with mechanical assembly and kitted lighting components.

### Scope

- Plastic production, pad program, mechanical close, and QC sampling.

- Kitting of brand-sourced LED strips with no custom harness build.

- Pack-out discipline and lot traceability for Phase 1 scaling.

Running start impact

- NRE driver:  first-article process setup and packaging proof dominate early launch cash.

- Landed driver:  print/material yields, assembly minutes, and QA allocation set per-unit cost.

- Deferrable to scale:  deeper automation and expanded process fixtures can be postponed until 100/1000-unit confidence.
